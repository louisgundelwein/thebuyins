## Description (App Store and Google Play full description, 2014/4000)
The Buy-Ins is a ledger for private home games among friends. It keeps track of who bought in, who rebought and who cashed out, and tells you at the end of the night who owes whom.

The app only keeps records. It does not move money, it does not offer betting or gambling of any kind, there are no odds, no payment links and no in-app purchases. Players settle up among themselves, in cash or however they like. Think of it as a notepad that does the maths.

HOW A GAME WORKS
- Create a group for your regular round and set a default buy-in in euros.
- Start a game and pick who is playing.
- Everyone is in for the default buy-in. Tap to add a rebuy whenever someone reloads. Added one by mistake? Remove it from the list.
- When a player is out, record their cash-out. The pot and every stack are always visible.
- Draw seats at random for the table. Not happy with it? Draw again.
- Settle: enter the chips each player ends with. If the numbers do not match the pot, the app can spread the difference fairly across the cash-outs so the books balance.
- See the result per player and a short list of who pays whom, using as few payments as possible. Share it with the group in one tap.

GROUPS AND FRIENDS
- Invite people with a link or a six-character code.
- Friends without an account can be added as guests by the host. When they sign up later, they can take over their history.
- Switch between several groups, for example Friday poker and the office round.

HISTORY AND STATS
- Every finished game is stored with all buy-ins and results.
- A leaderboard shows who is ahead over time.
- A balance chart shows how each player's total has developed from game to game.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Sign up with email and password. Your name is what the group sees.
- Amounts are in euros.
- You can sign out at any time from the profile menu.
- Dark design, built for use at the table.

The Buy-Ins is intended for adults, 18 and older. It is not a casino app and not a game. There is nothing to win or lose inside the app.
